DENVique Desktop Monitor must stay open and activated whenever you access a DENVique workspace, including outside scheduled shifts. Outside a scheduled shift, it records only app connection status and timestamps; no screenshots or app / website activity are collected. During an assigned employee shift, it collects screen snapshots every 5 minutes, foreground application names and website domains. It does not record keystrokes or separately read private messages or page contents. However, screenshots can show anything visible on your display, including messages, page content or sensitive information. Do not leave passwords, credentials or unnecessary sensitive information visible. Camera and microphone are not used.
Desktop Monitor is connected. During your scheduled employee shift, screen snapshots and foreground application / website-domain tracking are active. Outside your shift and during recorded breaks, this content collection is paused.
Desktop Monitor stays connected during your recorded break, but screen and activity capture remain paused.

Browser activity extension

During scheduled employee shifts only, the app can read the active website domain in Safari, Chrome or Edge on macOS after you grant browser automation permission. On Windows, install the companion extension in Chrome or Microsoft Edge. It records domains only, not page contents, passwords, messages or full URLs. This website-domain tracking is paused outside shifts and during breaks.

On Windows, website-domain tracking in Chrome or Edge requires this extension. Application names and screen captures are also shift-only.
